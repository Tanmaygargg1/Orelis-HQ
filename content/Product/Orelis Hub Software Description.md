### Why the business side feels weak

The individual side feels strong because the features are **experiential** — you can feel what they do. "An AI mentor that remembers every idea you've ever had" is something you can imagine. "Idea validation engine" gives you a picture.

The business side feels weak because the features are **architectural** — you've described the plumbing, not the experience. "Business intelligence engine" and "proactive weekly briefing" are things engineers write in a spec doc. They don't make a business owner feel anything.

### The business side, made strong

**What a business owner actually experiences:**

Every Monday morning, without asking for anything, they open Hub and find a briefing that says: _"Your three largest deals have been stalled for 14 days. Your best-performing customer segment dropped engagement by 9% this week. Your margins are healthy but your CAC is creeping up — here's why and what to do about it."_

That's not a dashboard. That's a consultant who did their homework over the weekend and is ready at 9am on Monday.

When they face a decision — should I hire, should I raise prices, should I drop this client — they don't Google it. They ask Hub. And Hub already knows their revenue, their margins, their team capacity, their pipeline. The answer isn't generic. It's theirs.

When they upload a contract, a competitor's pricing page, or their last quarter's P&L, Hub reads it, connects it to everything it already knows about the business, and tells them what it means for the decision they're currently wrestling with.

That's the pitch. Not "business intelligence engine." **A consultant that never sleeps, never bills by the hour, and already knows your business before you open your mouth.**

### The two sides are not two products

Here's the reframe that makes everything click:

**Individuals** are people at stage one of the same journey businesses are at stage three of. They're not a different customer — they're an earlier version of the same customer.

The pitch for both: _"Wherever you are in building something, Orelis is the strategic partner that should have existed from day one."_

For the individual: we help you think like a founder before you are one. For the business: we give you the strategic intelligence that used to be reserved for companies ten times your size.

Same firm. Same promise. Different stage of the same journey.
