### **🔧 Business Section Fix — Core Summary**

The business side is currently weaker because it is **feature-led instead of intelligence-led**. It lacks a strong onboarding and context-building system, meaning the AI does not deeply understand the company before giving value.

### **Key Problem**

- No structured “AI understanding phase” for companies
- AI Copilot is not grounded in real business context
- Features feel generic without company-specific intelligence
- Weak onboarding reduces activation and perceived value

### **Core Fix**

Introduce a **Business Intelligence Onboarding Layer before the dashboard unlocks**.

### **New Flow**

1. **Company Setup**
    - industry, size, stage, goals, pain points
2. **Data Connection Layer**
    
    - upload docs, SOPs, business info, resources  
        → AI builds a “Company Intelligence Profile”
3. **AI Business Model Creation**
    - AI generates structured understanding of the business:
        - strengths, weaknesses, opportunities
        - operational gaps
        - growth areas
4. **Dashboard Unlock**
    - All tools (Copilot, tasks, simulation) now become **context-aware**

### **Outcome**

- AI Copilot becomes truly personalized
- Tasks become business-specific (not generic)
- Simulations use real company context
- Platform feels like an AI business partner, not a tool

### **Core Shift**

From:

Feature-based business tools

To:

Context-aware AI business operating system
