# Getting Started — Orelis HQ (Team Guide)

Welcome to Orelis HQ. This is where the whole team stays aligned — content, ideas, tasks, and documentation all live here.

---

## Logging In

1. Open your browser and go to **hq.orelisstudios.com**
2. Type the team password and press **Enter**
3. You're in

That's it. No account to create, no email needed.

---

## The Two Sections

### Content
Everything written about Orelis — feature ideas, UI notes, marketing copy, strategy docs, handover notes. Think of it like a shared notebook.

- The **sidebar on the left** shows all files and folders
- Click any folder to open it
- Click any file to read it
- **Double-click** (or press the pencil icon) to edit a file
- Press **Cmd+S** (Mac) or **Ctrl+S** (Windows) to save your changes
- Click **Reading View** to go back to the clean read view

### Tasks
A kanban board for tracking work across the team.

- Three columns: **To Do**, **In Progress**, **Done**
- Click **Add task** to create a new one — give it a title, description, assignee, and due date
- **Drag a card** from one column to another to update its status
- Hover over a card and click the × to delete it

---

## Creating New Files and Folders

Look at the **Files** section in the left sidebar. You'll see two small icons next to the label:
- The **page icon** creates a new file
- The **folder icon** creates a new folder

Type a name and click Create. Files automatically get the `.md` extension (you don't need to type it).

---

## Writing in Files

Files are written in **Markdown** — a simple way of formatting text. Here's all you need to know:

| What you type | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| `# Heading` | Big heading |
| `## Heading` | Medium heading |
| `**bold**` | **bold** |
| `*italic*` | *italic* |
| `- item` | Bullet point |
| `[[Note Name]]` | Link to another note |

You can also type normally and it will just show as regular text.

---

## Tips

- **Reading View** is the default — it shows your content beautifully rendered
- **Double-click** anywhere on a file to start editing
- **Escape** or click "Reading View" to stop editing and go back to the clean view
- Your changes are only saved when you press **Cmd+S** or click the Save button
- If you see `[[note name]]` highlighted in gold — that's a link to another note in the system
