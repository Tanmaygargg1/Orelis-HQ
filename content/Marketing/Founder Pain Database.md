# Founder Pain Database

> This is a database for the pain points of founders and enrepreneuers that we have collected

Solopreneuers:

-   Finding customers to sell to
    
-   Managing time on business with other commitments
    
-   No idea on how to progress from current stage to next (need ROUTE not reccomendation)
    
-   Actually finding issues to solve
    
    -   The desire to build is there, but the pain point to tackle is unclear
        
-   Whether an idea is really worth pursuing and whether there is a market to penetrate and monetize
    
-   Trapped in day-to-day work, and cannot identify bottlenecks
    
    -   Do not focus on work of the highest leverage
        

SMB Owners:

-   Cash flow visibility, and data on financial decision timing
    
-   Drowning in admin and cash work instead of focusing on CRM
    
-   Drowning in tool sprawl -> too many tools that all work independently, and thus the owner is the glue for all the tools
    
-   Adopting AI but unable to integrate into systems
    

Freelancers:

-   Late payments -> loss of money
    

Creators:

-   Hit by algorithm issues, and inconsistent sponsorship/partnerships -> struggle to keep viewership etc. afloat, mostly content burnout
    

NICHE:

1.  Informal caregivers of ageing
    

-   The entire burden of taking care of someone elderly (bills, medicines, doctor visits, nutrition etc.) all falls under one apprentice of the elderly -> burnout, unnecessary burden
    

2.  Families underestimate long term crisis cost, and are only hit when it actually comes
    

-   Causes panic, and underpreparedness for any issue and crisis that could occur -> detrimental impacts on family
    

3.  SME succession -> silver tsunami
    

-   Global issue
    

4.  Healthcare professionals true work blocked by inbox overload -> blocks true healthcare work
    

5.  Small businesses struggle to find the vendors for their needs that actually deliver in their price range
    

6.  Ecommerce/fashion brands bleed money from poor fit + returns
    

Biggest note:

-   Build horizontally instead of vertically
    
-   Find ONE issue and capitalise on it
