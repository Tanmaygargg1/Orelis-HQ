very hard and tedious process of onboarding new consultants for every single job, especially for small businesses. 

orelis solves that by retaining a persistent memory of the entire business. 
every single convo, every single piece of info, every document.

then, it connects businesses to live business consultants on the platform itself -> who already have access to all the company's preexisting memory.
simply connect them to your project on the app, and youre done.
no fuss, no hassle, no difficulties.
