# workflows-and-features

CURRENT 'DREAM' FRAMEWORK: GPS FOR ENTREPRENEURSHIP

true workflow ideal (from feedback)

at onset -> user selects a destination

examples:

-   validate business idea
    
-   get first paying customer
    
-   reach $1k MRR
    
-   reach $10k MRR
    
-   launch product
    
-   expand business
    
-   raise funding
    

once destination is selected -> entire roadmap is shown (in a visual representation), as a map, path, or journey towards that destination

instead of only showing the next action, users can see:

where they currently are

what the next checkpoint is

what comes after that

why the checkpoint matters

what success looks like

what failure looks like

* * *

this path is then separated into checkpoints

examples:

Validate Idea  
↓  
Validate Demand  
↓  
First Paying Customers  
↓  
Repeat Customers  
↓  
Operational Consistency  
↓  
Scale

the goal is to move from checkpoint to checkpoint

each checkpoint contains:

-   objective
    
-   why it matters
    
-   recommended route
    
-   alternative routes
    
-   success criteria
    
-   failure criteria
    
-   expected timeline
    

* * *

rather than saying:

"ignore manufacturing"

it becomes:

Current Priority:  
Demand Validation

Future Priorities:  
Manufacturing  
Distribution  
Scaling

Reason:  
Without proven demand, manufacturing improvements create little value.

this gives context behind the prioritisation

* * *

if there are caveats, the system itself, like a GPS, adjusts the route accordingly

example:

Checkpoint:  
Validate Demand

Outcome A:  
Demand Validated

→ move to First Paying Customers

Outcome B:  
Weak Demand

→ investigate objections  
→ improve product  
→ test alternative customer segment

Outcome C:  
No Demand

→ test adjacent product  
→ revisit problem being solved

the user is never left with:  
"what now?"

the system should always provide a next route

* * *

rather than:

"here's your next task"

the product becomes:

"here's where you are"

"here's where you're trying to go"

"here's the most efficient route"

"here's what happens if this works"

"here's what happens if it doesn't"
